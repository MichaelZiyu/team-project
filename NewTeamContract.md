# Team Contract

## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, active communication, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---

## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

- **Communication channels:** Discord or text messages will be our primary communication channels.
- **Response time:** Team members are expected to respond to messages within approximately 24 hours.
- **Advance notification:** Members should notify the team as early as possible if they anticipate missing a deadline, lecture, or scheduled team activity due to illness, scheduling conflicts, extended holidays, or other circumstances.
- **Active discussion:** Team members are expected to actively participate in discussions, listen to each other's ideas, and give everyone a reasonable opportunity to meaningfully contribute.
- **Clarification and collaboration:** Team members should not expect to independently resolve every implementation or architectural question within their assigned tasks. There are often decisions and dependencies that require discussion with other members. Raise questions, concerns, and uncertainties in the group chat rather than making assumptions that could affect other components.

### Other Behaviours

- Actively contribute ideas, programming work, and constructive feedback.
- Be constructive and respectful when reviewing or discussing other people's work.
- Avoid unnecessary force pushes or other Git operations that could disrupt teammates' work.
- Respect agreed-upon responsibilities and the boundaries between different components.
- **Respect agreed-upon interfaces:** All implementations must follow the interfaces agreed upon by the team. If a member believes an interface is unsuitable, insufficient, or needs modification, they should raise the issue for group discussion and obtain agreement before making changes or adopting an alternative. Implementations that bypass or disregard agreed-upon interfaces without prior discussion and agreement will not be considered valid contributions to the project.
- **Take ownership while maintaining collaboration:** Members are responsible for implementing their assigned components, but this does not mean working in isolation. Actively discuss dependencies, integration details, architectural decisions, and unclear requirements with relevant teammates. Do not assume that every aspect of an assigned task can or should be decided independently.

---

## Decision Making

- Decisions will generally be made through consensus in the group chat.
- When a decision affects multiple components or team members' responsibilities, the relevant members should participate in the discussion before implementation.
- Proposed changes to agreed-upon interfaces, architecture, or responsibilities must be discussed and agreed upon by the affected team members before being implemented.
- Members should explain their reasoning when proposing technical decisions and remain open to constructive feedback.

---

## Conflict Resolution

- Listen to each other's perspectives and attempt to reach a reasonable compromise.
- Address disagreements through direct and respectful discussion rather than making unilateral decisions.
- For technical disagreements, discuss the relevant requirements, interfaces, and architectural implications before deciding on an approach.
- If a resolution cannot be reached, consult a TA or instructor as a team.

---

## Accountability

- Complete assigned work in a timely manner and seek assistance from teammates, TAs, or instructors when necessary.
- If a member anticipates that they cannot complete their assigned work on time, they are expected to notify the team in advance and explain the circumstances.
- Repeated failures to complete assigned responsibilities are expected to be compensated for through additional contributions elsewhere, so that the burden is not unreasonably transferred to other teammates.
- Members are responsible for ensuring that their contributions integrate with the team's agreed-upon architecture and interfaces.
- Completing an assigned task includes communicating with relevant teammates, clarifying requirements, and following agreed-upon interfaces. Merely producing an independent implementation that does not follow the team's agreed-upon design does not fulfill the responsibility.
- Members should communicate their progress, blockers, and integration issues so that problems can be identified and addressed early.
