## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Discord or text message.
* ~24 hours

* If they think they won't be able to meet a deadline, if they have to miss lecture, extended holiday in school time, sick, schedule conflict, absence

* Actively listening to each team members ideas, giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute, etc.

---

### Other Behaviours

* Actively contribute ideas and programming
* Don't git push force unnecessarily
* Be constructive about other people's work

---

## Decision Making

* Consensus vote in group chat

---
## Conflict resolution

* Listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.

---

## Accountability

* Completing their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required.
* If not completed, it is expected that the team be notified ahead of time, with good reason
* It is also expected that repeated failures be made up for elsewhere, so as to not cause unreasonable burden to other teammates
